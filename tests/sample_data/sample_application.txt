GreenTech Solutions Inc. proposes to develop an innovative manufacturing process for
perovskite solar cells that will reduce production costs by 40% while increasing energy

high-efficiency solar energy solutions. Our approach builds on three years of laboratory
research and aims to bridge the gap between lab-scale innovation and commercial-scale

Expected Outcomes:
- Scalable manufacturing process for perovskite solar cells
- 40% reduction in manufacturing costs compared to silicon-based cells
- Energy conversion efficiency exceeding 25%
- Path to commercial production within 3 years of project completion

TECHNICAL NARRATIVE

1. Technology Description
Perovskite solar cells represent one of the most promising advances in photovoltaic
technology. Our innovation focuses on a novel roll-to-roll manufacturing process that
enables continuous production of perovskite solar cells on flexible substrates.

The key innovation is our proprietary solvent-free deposition method, which eliminates
the need for toxic solvents used in conventional perovskite manufacturing. This method
achieves uniform film thickness across large areas, which is critical for commercial
viability.

2. Current State of Development
Our technology is currently at Technology Readiness Level (TRL) 4 — laboratory validation.
We have successfully demonstrated the solvent-free deposition method on 10cm x 10cm
substrates with conversion efficiencies of 22.3% (verified by the National Renewable
Energy Laboratory).

3. Research Methodology
Phase 1 (Months 1-8): Scale-up the deposition process from lab-scale to pilot-scale
  - Design and build pilot manufacturing line
  - Optimize process parameters for larger substrates (50cm x 50cm)
  - Conduct reliability and durability testing

Phase 2 (Months 9-16): Process optimization and testing
  - Fine-tune manufacturing parameters for maximum efficiency
  - Conduct accelerated aging tests (IEC 61215 standard)
  - Begin outdoor field testing of prototype modules

Phase 3 (Months 17-24): Demonstration and validation
  - Produce demonstration-scale modules
  - Install pilot solar array at partner facility
  - Collect performance data and prepare for commercialization

4. Timeline and Milestones
Month 3: Pilot line design complete
Month 8: First pilot-scale cells produced
Month 12: Efficiency target of 24% achieved on pilot-scale cells
Month 16: Durability testing complete
Month 20: Demonstration modules installed
Month 24: Final report and commercialization plan

5. Risk Assessment
Risk 1: Scale-up may reduce cell efficiency
  Mitigation: We have already achieved 90% efficiency retention in preliminary scale-up tests

Risk 2: Long-term stability of perovskite materials
  Mitigation: Our encapsulation technology has shown less than 5% degradation over 2000 hours of testing

Risk 3: Supply chain disruptions for raw materials
  Mitigation: We have identified multiple suppliers and developed material recycling processes
