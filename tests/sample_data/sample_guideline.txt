CLEAN ENERGY INNOVATION GRANT PROGRAM
Fiscal Year 2026 – Grant Guidelines

SECTION 1: PROGRAM OVERVIEW
The Clean Energy Innovation Grant Program (CEIGP) provides funding to support the development
and deployment of innovative clean energy technologies. The program aims to accelerate
the transition to renewable energy sources and reduce carbon emissions.

Funding Range: $50,000 to $500,000 per project
Project Duration: 12 to 36 months
Submission Deadline: December 15, 2026

SECTION 2: ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS (MANDATORY)

2.1 Organizational Eligibility
- Applicant must be a registered non-profit organization, academic institution, or small business (fewer than 500 employees)
- Organization must have been in operation for at least 2 years
- Applicant must be based in the United States
- Organization must have a DUNS number and active SAM.gov registration

2.2 Project Eligibility
- Project must focus on clean energy technology innovation
- Project must demonstrate potential for measurable carbon emission reduction
- Project cannot duplicate work already funded by federal grants
- Project must include a plan for technology commercialization or public benefit

2.3 Financial Requirements
- Applicant must provide a minimum 20% cost-share (matching funds)
- Organization must demonstrate financial stability (audited financial statements for the past 2 years)
- Budget must not exceed $500,000 in federal funding request

SECTION 3: APPLICATION COMPONENTS (MANDATORY)

3.1 Executive Summary (2 pages maximum)
- Clear description of the proposed project
- Statement of innovation and how it advances clean energy
- Expected outcomes and impact

3.2 Technical Narrative (15 pages maximum)
- Detailed description of the technology or innovation
- Current state of development (Technology Readiness Level)
- Research methodology and approach
- Timeline with key milestones
- Risk assessment and mitigation strategies

3.3 Budget and Budget Justification
- Detailed line-item budget using the provided template
- Budget justification for each line item
- Documentation of cost-share commitments

3.4 Organizational Capacity
- Description of key personnel and their qualifications
- Organizational experience with similar projects
- Facilities and equipment available for the project

3.5 Environmental Impact Assessment
- Projected carbon emission reductions
- Environmental benefits and potential risks
- Compliance with NEPA (National Environmental Policy Act)

SECTION 4: SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS (MANDATORY)

The following documents must be submitted with the application:
- Letters of support from project partners
- Resumes/CVs of key personnel (maximum 3 pages each)
- Audited financial statements (past 2 fiscal years)
- Proof of SAM.gov registration
- Cost-share commitment letters
- Institutional Review Board (IRB) approval, if applicable

SECTION 5: EVALUATION CRITERIA

Applications will be evaluated based on:
5.1 Innovation and Technical Merit (30 points)
5.2 Potential Impact and Carbon Reduction (25 points)
5.3 Feasibility and Project Plan (20 points)
5.4 Organizational Capacity and Team (15 points)
5.5 Budget Reasonableness (10 points)

SECTION 6: RECOMMENDATIONS (NOT MANDATORY)

The following are recommended but not required:
- Include preliminary data or proof-of-concept results
- Demonstrate community engagement or stakeholder support
- Provide letters of intent from potential technology adopters
- Include a sustainability plan for continuing the project beyond the grant period
- Describe plans for disseminating results through publications or conferences
- Consider including diverse team members and underrepresented communities in the project

SECTION 7: SUBMISSION INSTRUCTIONS

Applications must be submitted electronically through the Grants.gov portal.
All documents must be in PDF format.
Late submissions will not be accepted.
